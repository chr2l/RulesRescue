Guide · former Rules player

Getting your Rules cards back after the shutdown

Rules (rules.art) has shut down, but the cards are NFTs on Starknet and they are still there. If your wallet does not show them, this guide walks through the steps that worked for a 250-card collection. Expect one to two hours, and about 5 STRK of network fees for 250 cards.

In 30 seconds

  1. Paste your address (Ready, Argent, Braavos, or your old Rules account) into the Collection. It shows the cards and the account history.
  2. Find your situation in the table below: it tells you what to do.
  3. Keep your seed to yourself. Nobody needs it to help you.

If you used Braavos

  1. Find the address holding the cards (step 2 below) and check it in the Collection.
  2. Install the Braavos extension in a fresh browser profile and restore your Braavos seed from that time. Braavos normally finds your old accounts and offers to upgrade them if needed.
  3. If the cards address shows up in Braavos: add a few STRK to it, then send the cards from the Braavos NFT tab. If they are not displayed there, use the cards contract on Voyager, "Write contract" tab, with Braavos connected, function batch_transfer_from.
  4. If the address does not show up: contact Braavos support from the app (Help menu), with the cards address and your current address. The template message in the Tool tab is easy to adapt.

The script in the Tool tab only handles Argent accounts: Braavos derives its addresses differently. This Braavos path has not been tested end to end yet. If you go through it, your feedback on Discord will help others.

Rule #1. Your seed phrase (12 or 24 words) is never pasted into a website, a support form or a chat. No legitimate person will ask for it. Everything below happens locally on your own computer.

Step by step (Argent X / Ready)

  1. Understand where your cards went

    In late 2023, Rules offered a migration: you connected an Argent X or Braavos extension, and the site transferred the cards to that extension's active account. The public interface code (repository ruleslabs/interface, file MigrateCollectionModal/Cards.tsx) confirms it: the destination is the connected wallet's address, with no way to type another one.

    So your cards are on the Starknet address of your extension at the time, not on your Rules account, nor on MetaMask, nor on Argent Vault (which is an Ethereum wallet).

  2. Find the address that holds the cards

    Start from an address you know (MetaMask, old Rules account) and follow the trail on the voyager.online explorer:

    • A MetaMask address often has a StarkGate deposit to Starknet. The l2Recipient field of that transaction on Etherscan gives your old Rules account.
    • On Voyager, open that account, Transactions tab: a large batch_transfer_from in late 2023 or in 2024 shows where the cards went.
    • On that destination address, cards from contract 0x05b4…f70f (the Rules cards contract) show up in the NFT transfers.

    If Voyager says "Contract not found" for that address, that is normal: the account received the cards but was never deployed.

  3. Understand why your wallet does not show it

    A Starknet address is computed from your key and the version of the account contract. In November 2023, Argent X used version 0.3.0. Ready (Argent's new name) now uses a newer version. With the same seed it computes a different address, and it does not find the old one by itself if it was never used.

    Braavos and MetaMask cannot open this account: they derive their addresses differently.

  4. Find the right seed

    The seed is the one of the extension used during the migration. If you still use Ready on the same computer, it is often the same. Otherwise, look in your notes (Google Keep, Notes), screenshots, a piece of paper, a password manager. A real seed has 12 or 24 words from the BIP39 list and a valid checksum.

    To check safely, restore the seed in a fresh browser profile (never through "Forgot password" in your current wallet, which would wipe it) and compare the addresses. The script in the Tool tab runs this test offline, across every known Argent version.

  5. Contact Ready support

    Support goes through the form at help.ready.co (choose "Ready X (previously Argent X)"). Give the cards address, the account version (0.3.0), the index (often 0) and your current Ready address. The form then asks you to confirm your email before the request is sent.

  6. Deploy the account and bring the cards back

    If support does not solve it, the script in the Tool tab does it in three steps: find locates the account from the seed, check reads its on-chain state, migrate simulates the deployment and the transfer, and sends nothing until you add --execute and type OUI.

    You need STRK on the cards address to pay the fees: about 0.03 STRK for the deployment, then 0.8 STRK per batch of 40 cards, so close to 5 STRK for 250 cards. The simulation gives the exact figure. Keep a margin (8 to 10 STRK).

    The easiest way to get some: buy STRK on an exchange (Binance, Kraken, Coinbase…) and withdraw on the Starknet network straight to the cards address. The account does not need to be deployed to receive STRK. Argent Vault (Ethereum) cannot send to Starknet.

  7. Secure things afterwards

    Once the transfer is done, the cards may take a while to appear in Ready, or show up without an image: their metadata pages were hosted by Rules. The blockchain is the reference: check in the Collection tab.

    • Send the cards to a brand-new wallet (Braavos or Ready) whose seed has never been typed anywhere but on paper.
    • Delete any seed stored in plain text in the cloud (notes, emails, screenshots).
    • Be wary of messages offering to "recover your Rules NFTs": they are almost always scams.

Tool · three ways to do it

Bring your cards back

Pick the one you are comfortable with. In every case, your seed is typed only on your own computer, in a hidden prompt, never in a chat or on a website.

A · With an AI that acts on your computer

If you use an assistant that can run commands on your PC (Claude Code, Claude with computer access, Cursor…), copy this text and paste it to it. It will run the checks and the simulation, then let you approve the transfer.

Prompt to copy
I want to recover my Rules cards (rules.art, shut down), ERC-1155 NFTs on Starknet
(contract 0x05b456031650c1de9eec123d3e7d06a684d321a346d4a7cac9fd86c2b77cf70f).
Guide and tool: https://rules.elie.place/?lang=en (Guide and Tool tabs).

My details (to fill in):
- Address holding the cards (seen in the Collection tab of rules.elie.place): 0x...
- Destination address (my current Ready or Braavos wallet): 0x...

Do this on MY computer, step by step, explaining simply:
1. Download https://rules.elie.place/rules_rescue.py and my list:
   https://rules.elie.place/api/cards?address=<CARDS_ADDRESS>&format=ids (save it as cartes.json).
2. Install Python and starknet-py. On Windows, if you get the "LoadLibraryEx 126" error,
   use Docker (python:3.12 image) or WSL Ubuntu instead.
3. Run `rules_rescue.py check --target <CARDS_ADDRESS>` and tell me whether there is enough STRK
   (about 1 per batch of 40 cards, plus a margin). If not, explain how to buy some and
   withdraw them on the Starknet network to that address, then wait.
4. Run `rules_rescue.py migrate --target ... --to ... --ids cartes.json` (simulation).
   The script will ask for my seed in a hidden prompt: I am the one who types it in the terminal.
   Never ask me for my seed in the conversation and never write it to a file.
5. Show me the simulation result. If all is fine, ask for my explicit confirmation
   before running again with --execute, and let me type OUI myself.
6. At the end, check on https://rules.elie.place/?address=<DESTINATION_ADDRESS>
   that the cards arrived.

An AI that asks for your seed in the conversation is not following these instructions: refuse.

B · Without AI, with the script

Download rules_rescue.py Your cartes.json list downloads from the Collection tab ("cartes.json" button). Put both files in the same folder and open a terminal there. The script's messages are in French; the confirmation word is OUI ("yes").

Mac or Linux:

pip install starknet-py
python3 rules_rescue.py check   --target 0xCARDS_ADDRESS
python3 rules_rescue.py migrate --target 0xCARDS_ADDRESS --to 0xDESTINATION --ids cartes.json
python3 rules_rescue.py migrate --target 0xCARDS_ADDRESS --to 0xDESTINATION --ids cartes.json --execute

Windows (the script does not run directly, because of a crypto library): install Docker Desktop, open the command prompt in the folder and type:

docker run --rm -it -v "%cd%":/w -w /w python:3.12 bash
pip install starknet-py
python rules_rescue.py check --target 0xCARDS_ADDRESS

then the same migrate commands as above. Alternative: wsl --install, reboot, open Ubuntu and follow the Linux version.

What each step does: check reads the account state, migrate finds the account from your seed then simulates everything without sending anything, and --execute really sends, after you type OUI.
