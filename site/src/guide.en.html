Guide and tool · former Rules player

Getting your Rules cards back after the shutdown

Rules (rules.art) has shut down, but the cards are NFTs on Starknet and they are still there. What to do depends on the wallet you used during the Rules migration in late 2023: pick it below.

Rule #1. Your seed phrase (12 or 24 words) and your private key are never pasted into a website, a support form or a chat. No legitimate person will ask for them. Everything below happens locally on your own computer.

From which wallet?

Don't remember? Paste your old Rules account into the Collection: the history shows how many cards were sent. On Voyager, the large transfer from late 2023 gives the destination address. If no card ever left, you are in the "Rules wallet" case.

Still looking

You never migrated: your cards stayed on your original Rules account. They show in the Collection, but we do not have a working method yet to get them out. Here is honestly where things stand.

Why it is stuck

The original Rules account only accepts an old transaction format ("v1"). Starknet now only accepts the recent format ("v3"). So this account can no longer send anything: no transfer, no upgrade, no key change. Tested on 30/10/2026.

The lead we are digging into

  1. The account can no longer send a transaction, but it can still sign a message, offline.
  2. The Rules marketplace executes sell orders signed offline: the seller signs, the buyer pays the fees and the card moves to them.
  3. The cards contract lets the marketplace transfer a card without any other approval.
  4. The idea: sign with your Rules key an order "this card for 0 STRK", then execute it from your current wallet with a normal transaction. The card would leave the old account without that account having to send anything.

What is checked, what is missing

  • Checked in Rules' public code: every link above. The signature computation matches the official test in the Rules repository.
  • Checked on-chain on 05/11/2026: the cards contract still points to the Rules marketplace (0x01fea6…9937).
  • Not tested yet: a real simulation with an original Rules account that still holds cards and its private key exported from rules.art. Until that test is done, nothing can be guaranteed.
  • If you never used the rules.art key export (email login only), this lead will not work for you: only Rules Labs, which owns the contracts, could unlock those accounts.

Did you keep your exported key?

You can help us test: the simulation runs on your own computer, sends nothing and costs nothing. Write through Contact and just say you have an original Rules account with its key. Never put the key in the message: you will type it yourself into the script, locally.

Tested end to end

This is the method that worked: 252 cards moved from the old version of the Argent X account to Ready on 30/10/2026, for about 4.8 STRK of fees. Expect one to two hours.

Step by step (Argent X / Ready)

  1. Understand where your cards went

    In late 2023, Rules offered a migration: you connected an Argent X or Braavos extension, and the site transferred the cards to that extension's active account. The public interface code (repository ruleslabs/interface, file MigrateCollectionModal/Cards.tsx) confirms it: the destination is the connected wallet's address, with no way to type another one.

    So your cards are on the Starknet address of your extension at the time, not on your Rules account, nor on MetaMask, nor on Argent Vault (which is an Ethereum wallet).

  2. Find the address that holds the cards

    Start from an address you know (MetaMask, old Rules account) and follow the trail on the voyager.online explorer:

    • A MetaMask address often has a StarkGate deposit to Starknet. The l2Recipient field of that transaction on Etherscan gives your old Rules account.
    • On Voyager, open that account, Transactions tab: a large batch_transfer_from in late 2023 or in 2024 shows where the cards went.
    • On that destination address, cards from contract 0x05b4…f70f (the Rules cards contract) show up in the NFT transfers.

    If Voyager says "Contract not found" for that address, that is normal: the account received the cards but was never deployed.

  3. Understand why your wallet does not show it

    A Starknet address is computed from your key and the version of the account contract. In November 2023, Argent X used version 0.3.0. Ready (Argent's new name) now uses a newer version. With the same seed it computes a different address, and it does not find the old one by itself if it was never used.

    Same seed, account #0Address
    Argent X v0.3.0 (late 2023)the one holding the cards
    Ready v0.4.0 (today)the one the app shows

    Braavos and MetaMask cannot open this account: they derive their addresses differently.

  4. Find the right seed

    The seed is the one of the extension used during the migration. If you still use Ready on the same computer, it is often the same. Otherwise, look in your notes (Google Keep, Notes), screenshots, a piece of paper, a password manager. A real seed has 12 or 24 words from the BIP39 list and a valid checksum.

    To check safely, restore the seed in a fresh browser profile (never through "Forgot password" in your current wallet, which would wipe it) and compare the addresses. The tool below runs this test offline, across every known Argent version.

  5. Contact Ready support

    Support goes through the form at help.ready.co (choose "Ready X (previously Argent X)"). Give the cards address, the account version (0.3.0), the index (often 0) and your current Ready address. The form then asks you to confirm your email before the request is sent.

  6. Deploy the account and bring the cards back

    If support does not solve it, the tool below does it in three steps: find locates the account from the seed, check reads its on-chain state, migrate simulates the deployment and the transfer, and sends nothing until you add --execute and type OUI.

    You need STRK on the cards address to pay the fees: about 0.03 STRK for the deployment, then 0.8 STRK per batch of 40 cards, so close to 5 STRK for 250 cards. The simulation gives the exact figure. Keep a margin (8 to 10 STRK).

    The easiest way to get some: buy STRK on an exchange (Binance, Kraken, Coinbase…) and withdraw on the Starknet network straight to the cards address. The account does not need to be deployed to receive STRK. Argent Vault (Ethereum) cannot send to Starknet.

  7. Secure things afterwards

    Once the transfer is done, the cards may take a while to appear in Ready, or show up without an image: their metadata pages were hosted by Rules. The blockchain is the reference: check in the Collection tab.

    • Send the cards to a brand-new wallet (Braavos or Ready) whose seed has never been typed anywhere but on paper.
    • Delete any seed stored in plain text in the cloud (notes, emails, screenshots).
    • Be wary of messages offering to "recover your Rules NFTs": they are almost always scams.

Tool · move your cards from Argent X to Ready

This tool does one thing: move your cards from the old Argent X address (version 0.3.0, the one used for the Rules migration) to your current Ready wallet. It works neither for Braavos nor for an original Rules account. Pick the way that suits you. In every case, your seed is typed only on your own computer, in a hidden prompt.

B · Without AI, with the script

Download rules_rescue.py Your cartes.json list downloads from the Collection tab ("cartes.json" button). Put both files in the same folder and open a terminal there. The script's messages are in French; the confirmation word is OUI ("yes").

Mac or Linux:

pip install starknet-py
python3 rules_rescue.py check   --target 0xCARDS_ADDRESS
python3 rules_rescue.py migrate --target 0xCARDS_ADDRESS --to 0xDESTINATION --ids cartes.json
python3 rules_rescue.py migrate --target 0xCARDS_ADDRESS --to 0xDESTINATION --ids cartes.json --execute

Windows (the script does not run directly, because of a crypto library): install Docker Desktop, open the command prompt in the folder and type:

docker run --rm -it -v "%cd%":/w -w /w python:3.12 bash
pip install starknet-py
python rules_rescue.py check --target 0xCARDS_ADDRESS

then the same migrate commands as above. Alternative: wsl --install, reboot, open Ubuntu and follow the Linux version.

What each step does: check reads the account state, migrate finds the account from your seed then simulates everything without sending anything, and --execute really sends, after you type OUI.

Not tested

If you did the Rules migration with Braavos, your cards are on your Braavos account from that time. Nobody has gone through this path end to end yet, so what follows is the method that should work, with no guarantee. The script from the Argent X tab does not apply: Braavos derives its addresses differently.

  1. Find the cards address

    Paste your old Rules account into the Collection, then on Voyager spot the large card transfer from late 2023: the destination is your Braavos account from that time. Check it in the Collection too.

  2. Restore Braavos in a fresh profile

    Install the Braavos extension in a fresh browser profile and restore your Braavos seed from that time. Do not use "Forgot password" on your current wallet: it wipes it. Braavos normally finds your old accounts and offers to upgrade them if needed: accept the upgrade.

  3. Add a few STRK

    Send 2 to 3 STRK to the cards address, from an exchange (withdrawal on the Starknet network) or another Starknet wallet. They pay for the fees.

  4. Send the cards

    From the Braavos NFT tab, send the cards to your current wallet. If they are not displayed there, use the cards contract on Voyager, "Write contract" tab, with Braavos connected, function batch_transfer_from.

  5. If the address does not show up in Braavos

    Contact Braavos support from the app (Help menu) with the message below.

Message to Braavos support
Hello,

In late 2023 I migrated my Rules (rules.art) trading cards to my Braavos account on Starknet.
The cards (ERC-1155, contract 0x05b456031650c1de9eec123d3e7d06a684d321a346d4a7cac9fd86c2b77cf70f)
are on this address:
0x... (cards address)

After restoring my seed, Braavos does not show this account. My current Braavos address is 0x...
How can I access this legacy account and transfer my NFTs?
I will never share my seed phrase.

Thank you

Why it should work: unlike the original Rules account, a Braavos account is a regular wallet that Braavos can still upgrade, and sending the cards is a normal transfer. What we don't know: whether a 2023 Braavos account left unused since then restores and upgrades smoothly. If you go through it, say so with the button at the bottom of the page: it will help others.
