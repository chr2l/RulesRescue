Guide and tool · former Rules player

Getting your Rules cards back

Rules has shut down, but your cards still exist on the Starknet blockchain. How to get them back depends on the wallet you used in late 2023.

Rule #1: your seed stays secret. Your seed (your 12 or 24 words) and your private key are never pasted into a website or a chat. No serious person will ask for them.
The tricky words, explained simply
Wallet
The app that holds your crypto and your cards (Ready, Argent X, Braavos…).
Seed
The 12 or 24 words given when the wallet was created. It is the key to everything: whoever has it can take it all.
Address
A wallet's public "account number", starting with 0x. Safe to share.
STRK
Starknet's currency. It pays the fee for each operation (a few cents to a few euros).
Deploying an account
Activating it on the blockchain. An account can receive cards without being active, but must be active to send them.
Voyager
A website showing everything that happens on Starknet: accounts, transfers, cards.
Terminal
The window where you type commands ("Command Prompt" on Windows, "Terminal" on Mac).

Which wallet did you migrate with?

Don't remember? Paste your old Rules account into the Collection. If cards left it in late 2023, the destination is your wallet from that time. If nothing left, pick "Rules wallet".

Still looking

You never migrated: your cards stayed on your original Rules account. We can see them, but we do not have a working method yet to get them out.

Why it is stuck

This account only understands an old transaction format. Starknet no longer accepts it. As a result the account cannot send anything, even with its key.

The lead we are digging into

  • The account can no longer send, but it can still sign (say "yes, I allow this").
  • The Rules marketplace accepts a sale signed in advance: the seller signs, the buyer executes.
  • The idea: sign with your Rules key "I sell this card for 0", then buy it from your current wallet. The card would arrive on your side.

Where we are

  • Rules' public code does allow this path.
  • The marketplace is still wired to the cards contract (checked on 05/11/2026).
  • Never tested for real: we need a former player who kept their private key exported from rules.art.
  • If you logged into Rules by email and never exported your key, this lead will not work. Only Rules Labs could unlock those accounts.

Tested end to end

This is the method that worked: 252 cards moved to Ready on 30/10/2026, for about 4.8 STRK of fees.

What you need

  • The seed of the Argent X wallet you had in late 2023.
  • About 8 to 10 STRK for fees (5 STRK for 250 cards, plus a margin).
  • A computer, and one to two hours.

The steps

  1. Understand where your cards are

    During the migration, Rules sent your cards to the address of your Argent X extension at the time. They are neither on your Rules account nor on MetaMask.

  2. Find the cards address

    • Paste your old Rules account into the Collection.
    • On Voyager, open that account: a large card transfer in late 2023 shows the destination.
    • Paste that address into the Collection to check your cards are there.

    Voyager says "Contract not found"? That is normal: the account received the cards but was never activated.

  3. Why Ready does not show your cards

    An address depends on your seed and on the wallet version. With the same seed, the 2023 Argent X (version 0.3.0) and today's Ready (version 0.4.0) give two different addresses. Ready only shows the new one.

  4. Find the right seed

    It is the seed of the Argent X extension used during the migration. If you still use Ready on the same computer, it is often the same one.

    Otherwise, look in your notes, screenshots, a piece of paper or your password manager.

    To test a seed safely, use a fresh browser profile. Never use "Forgot password" in your current wallet: it wipes it.

  5. Add STRK to the cards address

    Buy STRK on an exchange (Binance, Kraken, Coinbase…) and withdraw them on the Starknet network, straight to the cards address. It does not need to be active to receive.

  6. Run the tool

    The tool just below activates the account and sends the cards to your Ready, 40 at a time. It runs a simulation first and sends nothing without your OK.

  7. Check and stay safe

    • Check the arrival in the Collection. In Ready, cards may show without an image: that is normal, the images were hosted by Rules.
    • Delete any seed stored in plain text in the cloud (notes, emails, screenshots).
    • Be wary of messages offering to "recover your Rules NFTs": they are almost always scams.

The tool: move your cards from Argent X to Ready

It does one thing: move your cards from the old Argent X address to your current Ready. Pick the way that suits you.

A · With an AI that acts on your computer

If you have an assistant that can run commands on your PC (Claude Code, Cursor…), paste it this text. It will do the checks and the simulation, then let you confirm.

I want to recover my Rules cards (rules.art, shut down), ERC-1155 NFTs on Starknet
(contract 0x05b456031650c1de9eec123d3e7d06a684d321a346d4a7cac9fd86c2b77cf70f).
Guide and tool: https://rulesrescue.elie.place/?lang=en (Guide tab, Argent X part).

My details (to fill in):
- Address holding the cards (seen in the Collection tab of rulesrescue.elie.place): 0x...
- Destination address (my current Ready or Braavos wallet): 0x...

Do this on MY computer, step by step, explaining simply:
1. Download https://rulesrescue.elie.place/rules_rescue.py and my list:
   https://rulesrescue.elie.place/api/cards?address=<CARDS_ADDRESS>&format=ids (save it as cartes.json).
2. Install Python and starknet-py. On Windows, if you get the "LoadLibraryEx 126" error,
   use Docker (python:3.12 image) or WSL Ubuntu instead.
3. Run `rules_rescue.py check --target <CARDS_ADDRESS>` and tell me whether there is enough STRK
   (about 1 per batch of 40 cards, plus a margin). If not, explain how to buy some and
   withdraw them on the Starknet network to that address, then wait.
4. Run `rules_rescue.py migrate --target ... --to ... --ids cartes.json` (simulation).
   The script will ask for my seed in a hidden prompt: I am the one who types it in the terminal.
   Never ask me for my seed in the conversation and never write it to a file.
5. Show me the simulation result. If all is fine, ask for my explicit confirmation
   before running again with --execute, and let me type OUI myself.
6. At the end, check on https://rulesrescue.elie.place/?address=<DESTINATION_ADDRESS>
   that the cards arrived.

An AI that asks for your seed in the conversation is not following the instructions: refuse.

B · Yourself, with the script

  1. Download rules_rescue.py
  2. Also download your cartes.json list from the Collection ("cartes.json" button).
  3. Put both files in the same folder and open a terminal in it.

On Mac or Linux, type these lines one by one:

pip install starknet-py
python3 rules_rescue.py check   --target 0xCARDS_ADDRESS
python3 rules_rescue.py migrate --target 0xCARDS_ADDRESS --to 0xDESTINATION --ids cartes.json
python3 rules_rescue.py migrate --target 0xCARDS_ADDRESS --to 0xDESTINATION --ids cartes.json --execute

On Windows, the script does not run directly. Install Docker Desktop, open the Command Prompt in the folder, then:

docker run --rm -it -v "%cd%":/w -w /w python:3.12 bash
pip install starknet-py
python rules_rescue.py check --target 0xCARDS_ADDRESS

Then the same migrate lines as for Mac.

CommandWhat it does
checkLooks at the account and how many STRK it has. Asks for nothing.
migrateAsks for your seed (hidden input), finds the account and simulates everything. Sends nothing.
--executeSends for real, after you type OUI.

Common problems
Windows: "LoadLibraryEx 126" error at launch
Normal on Windows: use Docker (above) or WSL (wsl --install, restart, open Ubuntu and follow the Linux version).
Ready only shows your current address
Normal: the cards are on the old version of the same account. The script finds it with your seed.
Argent Vault refuses to send to Starknet
Argent Vault is an Ethereum wallet. Go through an exchange and withdraw the STRK on the Starknet network.
Transfer refused, not enough fees
Add STRK to the cards address and run again: the script resumes where it stopped.

If you migrated with Braavos, your cards are on your Braavos account from that time. Nobody has gone all the way through this yet: here is what should work, with no guarantee.

  1. Find the cards address

    Paste your old Rules account into the Collection. On Voyager, the large card transfer in late 2023 shows your Braavos address from that time.

  2. Restore Braavos in a fresh profile

    Install Braavos in a fresh browser profile and enter your Braavos seed from that time. Braavos normally finds your old accounts. If it offers an account upgrade, accept it.

  3. Add 2 to 3 STRK

    Send them to the cards address, from an exchange (Starknet network) or another wallet. They pay the fees.

  4. Send the cards

    From the Braavos NFT tab, send them to your current wallet. If they do not show, use the cards contract on Voyager: "Write contract" tab, Braavos connected, function batch_transfer_from.

  5. If Braavos does not show the address

    Contact Braavos support from the app (Help menu) with this message:

Hello,

In late 2023 I migrated my Rules (rules.art) trading cards to my Braavos account on Starknet.
The cards (ERC-1155, contract 0x05b456031650c1de9eec123d3e7d06a684d321a346d4a7cac9fd86c2b77cf70f)
are on this address:
0x... (cards address)

After restoring my seed, Braavos does not show this account. My current Braavos address is 0x...
How can I access this legacy account and transfer my NFTs?
I will never share my seed phrase.

Thank you

Why it should work: a Braavos account is a regular wallet that Braavos can still upgrade. What we don't know: whether a 2023 account never used since restores smoothly. If you make it, say so with the "It worked" button!
